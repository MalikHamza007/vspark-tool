Scope: whole app (Operate mode) — shell, auth, dashboard, Caption Studio, Scheduled Feed, Notifications, Admin.
Audience/job: creators draft captions with Gemini, schedule, sync to Drive, get email reminders; judged in a short live demo.
Constraints: Angular 21 + Tailwind 4, keep every function, responsive to 360px, light ground.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The app is a riso-printed zine studio: captions are printed proofs you pick, not chat bubbles in cards. Refuses the SaaS-sidebar-with-gradient-hero default.
OWN-WORLD: Bright white uncoated paper (#fbfbf8) with faint grain; three inks only — riso black (#1a1a1a), teal (#00838a) for actions/live state, sunflower yellow (#ffd400) as highlighter field for the selected/active thing. Overprint shadow = 3px offset teal block behind ink-bordered panels. 2px ink rules, square corners, no blur shadows, no gradients.
STORY: See what goes out next (time is always explicit), generate 3 proofs, mark one, stamp it scheduled; the feed reads like a printed run sheet.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead bar (VSPARK wordmark in heavy condensed caps, issue line with today's date, nav as numbered tabs). Dashboard: giant condensed headline "Next out" with the next post's time at poster scale left; right column numbered stat strip; primary action "Write captions" as ink block button.
FORM: Riso zine studio, candidate 5 of 7, seed: fa92b50d6c00631a132e7e6f820d33d1-ref (seed: fa92b50d6c00631a).
Raises: visible 12-col grid rules on page headers (Crouwel discipline); dense edge-to-edge feed cells sharing borders (convention-catalog density); type as the interface, no decorative icons (Metro).
Signature interaction: choosing a caption proof slams a yellow highlighter band across it and a rotated "PICKED" stamp; status chips are rubber stamps.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
